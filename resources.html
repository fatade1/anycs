Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about eligibility, portal access, and activities.

To register as a Full Member, you must satisfy three statutory conditions:
  1. Be a Nigerian citizen not less than eighteen (18) years and not more than thirty-five (35) years of age as at the date of registration.
  2. Be an actively serving officer in any Ministry, Department or Agency (MDA) of the Federal Government, a State Government or a Local Government Council in Nigeria.
  3. Hold a valid letter of employment, appointment, or an official Staff identification document from your MDA.
Members who exit on account of age seamlessly transition into the Associate/ Alumni Member category. Alumni retain mentorship roles, participate in advisory bodies, and continue collaborating across national summits without losing their forum connection.
Track A projects are thematic national initiatives across 7 priority sectors (Education, Health, Agriculture, ICT, Governance, Energy, Industry) running across multiple states simultaneously. Track B projects are locally scoped and owned by individual State Chapters plus the FCT, addressing specific grassroots civil service needs in their state.
