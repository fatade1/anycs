Our Story

Nigeria Civil Service Youths Forum, a non-partisan professional body established to promote excellence, innovation, integrity and transformative leadership among young public servants across the Federal, States and Local Government Councils Public Service of Nigeria.

Vision & Mission

We envision a dynamic and united network of youths in public service who are committed to advancing national development through Professionalism, Collaboration and Innovative service delivery.

Core Foundation

Uniting the 3 tiers of Nigerian governance with cutting-edge civic capacity and institutional integrity.

Strategic Priorities

Core Objectives

To realise this vision, the Forum's work is anchored on the following five priorities:

01

Professional Development

Promoting continuous learning, through structured training, seminars, workshops and conferences.

02

Innovation & Digital Transformation

Encouraging researches, innovations and the adoption of technologies to improve service delivery in the public service sector.

03

Networking & Collaboration

Creating platforms that connect young public servants in Ministries, Departments and Agencies across the three tiers of Government.

04

Youth Empowerment

Advancing skills acquisition, ICT development and agro-allied initiatives in partnership with government institutions and relevant private sectors.

05

Policy Advocacy & Welfare

Championing policies and programmes that strengthen community services and welfare of the general public.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Requirement

Any Nigerian not less than eighteen (18) years and not more than thirty-five (35) years of age as at the date of registration.

Serving Officer

Is a serving officer in any Ministry, Department or Agency (MDA) of the Federal Government, a State Government or a Local Government Council in Nigeria.

Official Verification

Holds a valid letter of employment, appointment or an official Staff identification document from their MDA.

🏛️

Full Member

A serving civil servant aged 18–35 who has met all eligibility requirements.

  • ✓ Full voting and leadership eligibility
  • ✓ Priority access to structured seminars
  • ✓ Official membership credentials
Apply as Full Member
🎓

Associate/ Alumni Member

A former full member who has exited on account of age or has left the Civil Service in good standing.

  • ✓ Mentorship and advisory roles
  • ✓ Continued network collaboration
  • ✓ Invitations to national summits

Honorary Member

A distinguished individual conferred with membership by the National Executive Council in recognition of support to the Forum’s objectives.

National Implementation

Projects Framework

Structured delivery through thematic national sectors and locally scoped state initiatives.

Track A • Thematic & Multi-State

By Sector Projects

Sector projects are thematic and may run across multiple states simultaneously. The seven national sectors focus on critical public sector development:

  • 🌱 Education & Youth Capacity Development
  • 🏥 Health & Public Wellbeing
  • 🌾 Agriculture & Food Security
  • 💻 ICT, Innovation & Digital Skills
  • 🏛️ Governance & Civic Engagement
  • ⚡ Energy, Environment & Climate Resilience
  • 💼 Industry, Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment
Explore Track A Sectors →
Track B • Locally Scoped

By State Projects

State-track projects are locally scoped and owned by a State Chapter plus the FCT. These grassroots actions address localized administrative bottlenecks, grassroots capacity building, and community service initiatives in all 36 States and Abuja.

Grassroots Ownership

Coordinated by State Chapters in direct liaison with State Head of Service and local government service commissions.
