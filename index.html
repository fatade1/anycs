Who We Are

Championing Excellence in Public Service

The Association of Nigerian Youths in Civil Service (ANYCS) is a national body dedicated to empowering young civil servants with the skills, networks, and platforms they need to drive transformative change in governance and public administration.

🎓
Capacity Building

Equipping members with modern skills, certifications, and knowledge to excel in their roles and advance their careers in public service.

🤝
Mentorship

Connecting emerging leaders with seasoned professionals who provide guidance, support, and invaluable career insights.

🌐
Networking

Building a powerful network of young professionals across Ministries, Departments, and Agencies nationwide.

Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

◆ Our Vision

To be the foremost platform for the development, empowerment, and representation of young civil servants in Nigeria, fostering a generation of innovative, ethical, and transformative public sector leaders.

◆ Our Mission

To unite, equip, and empower young Nigerian civil servants through capacity building, mentorship, innovation, and advocacy — creating an enabling environment for professional growth and impactful public service delivery.

What We Stand For

Core Objectives

Our pillars of action that drive meaningful impact across Nigeria's public service landscape.

📚
Professional Development

Organizing training programs, workshops, and seminars that enhance the professional competence of young civil servants.

💡
Innovation & Research

Promoting innovative thinking, research, and the adoption of technology in public sector operations and service delivery.

💼
Income & Skill Development

Creating pathways for income generation, ICT proficiency, and entrepreneurial opportunities for members.

🔗
Networking

Building bridges between young civil servants across MDAs, fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing.

📢
Advocacy

Championing policies that promote youth inclusion, welfare, and advancement within the Nigerian civil service framework.

🏆
Leadership Development

Cultivating the next generation of public sector leaders through structured mentorship and leadership programs.
