Our Objectives

What We Strive to Achieve

Our strategic pillars of action that drive meaningful impact across Nigeria's public service landscape.

Objective 01

Training & Professional Development

We organize high-impact training programs, workshops, conferences, and seminars designed to sharpen the professional skills of our members and equip them for leadership roles in the civil service.

Our programs cover a wide range of topics including public administration best practices, project management, digital literacy, communication skills, and leadership development — all tailored to the unique needs of Nigerian civil servants.

  • ✓ Professional certification sponsorships
  • ✓ Quarterly skill-building workshops
  • ✓ Annual leadership conference
  • ✓ E-learning platform access
📚

Building Capacity

Equipping civil servants with skills that matter for the future of public service.

Objective 02

ICT & Agro-Allied Opportunities

In a world driven by technology and sustainable development, we expose our members to ICT-driven income streams, agricultural innovations, and entrepreneurial opportunities that complement their civil service careers.

From digital marketing and data analytics to agro-tech and sustainable farming practices, we create pathways for members to diversify their income and expert-led trainings on AI while contributing to national development goals.

  • ✓ ICT skills bootcamps
  • ✓ Agro-business partnerships
  • ✓ Digital entrepreneurship training
  • ✓ Income diversification workshops
💻

Tech & Agriculture

Creating pathways for sustainable income and technological competence.

Objective 03

Innovation & Research

We champion creative thinking, research-driven solutions, and the adoption of modern technologies in public sector operations. Our innovation programs encourage members to develop solutions that improve government efficiency and service delivery.

Through hackathons, research grants, and innovation challenges, we create platforms for young civil servants to showcase their creativity and contribute original ideas to improving governance.

  • ✓ Annual Innovation Challenge
  • ✓ Research publication support
  • ✓ Tech adoption advocacy
  • ✓ Smart governance initiatives
💡

Think. Create. Transform.

Driving innovation in governance through fresh ideas and modern technology.

Objective 04

Networking & Collaboration

Building a powerful, nationwide network of professionals committed to public service excellence.

🌐
Cross-MDA Connections

Connect with peers across different Ministries, Departments, and Agencies to share knowledge, best practices, and opportunities.

🎤
Events & Conferences

Regular meetups, conferences, and networking events that bring together young civil servants from across Nigeria.

🤝
Strategic Partnerships

Collaborations with international organizations, NGOs, and private sector entities to create more opportunities for members.

Objective 05

Policy Advocacy

We serve as the voice of young civil servants at the policy level. Through structured engagement with key stakeholders, legislative bodies, and government agencies, we advocate for policies and reforms that:

  • → Promote youth inclusion in civil service leadership positions
  • → Improve welfare packages and working conditions
  • → Create clear career advancement pathways
  • → Encourage merit-based recruitment and promotion
  • → Strengthen anti-corruption and transparency measures
📢

Your Voice Matters

Championing policies that put young civil servants at the center of Nigeria's governance transformation.
