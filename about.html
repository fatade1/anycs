About Us

Who We Are

Understanding the heart and soul behind Nigeria's foremost association for young civil servants.

Our Story

A Movement for Change

The Association of Nigerian Youths in Civil Service (ANYCS) was born out of the pressing need to create a unified platform where young Nigerians serving in various Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs) across all tiers of government can connect, grow, and lead.

We recognize that the Nigerian civil service is the backbone of governance, and young people within it are the driving force of transformation. Yet, many lack access to adequate training, mentorship, and the networks needed to thrive. ANYCS bridges that gap.

Our approach is simple but powerful: unite young civil servants, equip them with world-class skills, and position them as catalysts for efficient, innovative, and transparent public service delivery.

🇳🇬

Serving the Nation

From federal to state and local government levels, ANYCS members are at the forefront of public service improvement, policy implementation, and institutional reform across Nigeria.

Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

◆ Our Vision

To be the foremost platform for the development, empowerment, and representation of young civil servants in Nigeria — fostering a generation of innovative, ethical, and transformative public sector leaders who will shape the future of governance in Africa's largest democracy.

◆ Our Mission

To unite, equip, and empower young Nigerian civil servants through capacity building, mentorship, innovation, and advocacy — creating an enabling environment for professional growth, knowledge exchange, and impactful public service delivery at all levels of government.

Eligibility

Who Is ANYCS For?

Our membership is open to young Nigerians (18-39) actively serving or associated with the civil service at any tier of government.

🏛️
Federal Civil Servants

Young professionals serving in federal ministries, departments, agencies, and parastatals across Nigeria.

🏢
State Civil Servants

Youth working in state-level government establishments, state MDAs, and related public institutions.

🏘️
Local Government Staff

Young civil servants operating at the local government level, serving their communities from the grassroots.

🤝
Associate Members

Professionals in allied organizations, NGOs, and public-sector adjacent roles who share our vision for public service excellence.

🎓
NYSC Members

National Youth Service Corps members posted to government establishments who wish to build a career in civil service.

🌟
Aspiring Civil Servants

Young Nigerians preparing for or interested in a career in public service who want early exposure and mentorship.

Our Purpose

Why ANYCS Exists

Bridging the Gap

Many young civil servants enter the system with passion but lack the structured support to develop into impactful leaders. ANYCS provides the mentorship, training, and community needed to bridge this gap.

Driving Innovation

Nigeria's civil service needs fresh ideas and modern approaches. We encourage research, technology adoption, and innovative thinking to improve government operations and service delivery.

Amplifying Youth Voices

We advocate for policies that recognize the contributions of young civil servants, promote welfare, and create clear pathways for career advancement within the public sector.

Building a National Network

Through our state chapters and national programs, we create a powerful network that transcends geographical and institutional boundaries, fostering unity and collaboration.
