Our Story

Nigeria Civil Service Youths Forum

Nigeria Civil Service Youths Forum, a non-partisan professional body established to promote excellence, innovation, integrity and transformative leadership among young public servants across the Federal, States and Local Government Councils Public Service of Nigeria.

Operating across the three tiers of government, the Forum serves as a unifying bridge connecting young talents in ministries, extra-ministerial departments, and agencies (MDAs). By fostering cross-sectoral collaboration, we empower young officers with the competencies required to implement progressive public policies effectively.

Vision and Mission

Guiding our collective effort towards national prosperity and service excellence.

◆ Our Vision & Mission

"We envision a dynamic and united network of youths in public service who are committed to advancing national development through Professionalism, Collaboration and Innovative service delivery."

◆ Core Values

Our members adhere to the highest codes of civil service conduct:

  • Excellence: Delivering world-class public service without compromise.
  • Integrity: Upholding transparency and unyielding accountability.
  • Innovation: Pioneering digital solutions and process efficiency.
  • Transformative Leadership: Mentoring future public sector leaders.
🎯

Core Objectives

To realise this vision, the Forum's work is anchored on five strategic priorities: Professional Development, Innovation & Digital Transformation, Networking & Collaboration, Youth Empowerment, and Policy Advocacy & Welfare.
