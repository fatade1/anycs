Grassroots Delivery

State Chapters

Spanning all 36 States and the Federal Capital Territory, driving Track B localized initiatives.

Decentralized Grassroots Power

Each State Chapter operates with an elected Chapter Executive Committee that liaises directly with the State Head of Service, State Civil Service Commission, Local Government Service Commission, and federal secretariats stationed in the state.

  • State Chapter Coordinator: Coordinates all state-level activities and sits on the National Working Committee.
  • LGA Liaisons: Ensures young officers in local government councils are actively mobilized.
  • Track B Project Ownership: Proposes and implements locally tailored public sector reform and community service projects.
