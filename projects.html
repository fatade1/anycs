Track A • Thematic National Framework

Track A – By Sector (Thematic, Multi-State)

Sector projects are thematic and may run across multiple states simultaneously. The seven national sectors harness multi-disciplinary youth task forces from federal, state, and local agencies to drive systemic modernization.

National Priority Sector 01

Education and Youth Capacity Development

Modernizing civil service onboarding curricula, structured administrative apprenticeships, and digital literacy bootcamps across state and federal secretariats.

Participating States: FCT, Lagos, Kano, Enugu, Oyo, Rivers
National Priority Sector 02

Health and Public Wellbeing

Strengthening primary healthcare management in local government areas, civil servant wellness programs, health insurance sensitization, and medical outreach.

Participating States: Multi-State Primary Health Care Boards
National Priority Sector 03

Agriculture and Food Security

Empowering young civil servants with agricultural cooperatives, agro-allied value chains, modern storage practices, and food distribution monitoring.

Participating States: Benue, Niger, Kaduna, Oyo, Kebbi, Cross River
National Priority Sector 04

ICT, Innovation and Digital Skills

Pioneering GovTech tools, electronic records management, cybersecurity readiness, and automated workflow solutions for public agencies.

Participating States: National Digital Taskforce (36 States + FCT)
National Priority Sector 05

Governance and Civic Engagement

Deepening institutional transparency, ethics training, open budgeting initiatives, and public consultation townhalls across local councils.

Participating States: Cross-Tier Governance Working Group
National Priority Sector 06

Energy, Environment and Climate Resilience

Deploying renewable energy mini-grids in government facilities, environmental sanitation drives, tree planting, and climate adaptation frameworks.

Participating States: Rivers, Delta, Borno, Sokoto, Lagos, Plateau
National Priority Sector 07

Industry, Entrepreneurship and Economic Empowerment

Facilitating MSME regulatory ease, export documentation training for trade officers, and financial literacy programs for public sector staff.

Participating States: All 6 Geopolitical Zones
Grassroots Framework

Track B – By State (Locally Scoped)

State-track projects are locally scoped and owned by a State Chapter plus the FCT.

FCT Chapter ● Active

Federal Archives Digital Indexing

Voluntary digitization of policy circulars and service records across 14 Federal Ministries in the Abuja Central Business District.

Lead: FCT Young Administrative Officers Squad
Lagos State Chapter ● Active

Citizens' Service Experience Feedback Lab

Deploying QR code citizen feedback points across state vehicle licensing and registration offices to evaluate civil service responsiveness.

Lead: Lagos State Public Service Chapter
Kano State Chapter ● Active

LGA Records Modernization

Structured training workshops for local government council officers across 44 LGAs in revenue documentation and computerized filing.

Lead: Kano Chapter LGA Working Group
Rivers State Chapter ● Active

Coastal Environmental Sanitation Corps

Youth civil servant volunteer sanitation and tree planting drive in collaboration with the Rivers State Ministry of Environment.

Lead: Rivers State Chapter Green Initiative
Enugu State Chapter ● Active

Rural Health Outreach Liaison

Voluntary supply-chain monitoring for primary health centers across rural communities to reduce pharmaceutical delivery stockouts.

Lead: Enugu State Healthcare Taskforce
Oyo State Chapter ● Active

Agro-Allied Civil Servants Cooperative

Establishing structured maize and cassava farming clusters for young civil servants in Ibadan to improve secondary food sufficiency.

Lead: Oyo Chapter Agro-Allied Council
