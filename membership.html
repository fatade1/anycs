Membership Categories

We offer two membership categories to serve civil servants and allied professionals alike.

🏛️

Full Member

For active civil servants currently serving in any Nigerian MDA at federal, state, or local government level.

  • Full voting rights
  • Eligible for executive positions
  • Access to all programs & training
  • Mentorship matching
  • Priority event registration
  • Certificate of membership
Register as Full Member
🤝

Associate Member

For professionals in allied organizations, NGOs, and public-sector adjacent roles who share our vision.

  • Observer status at meetings
  • Access to networking events
  • Training program access
  • Newsletter & updates
  • Community forum access
  • Digital membership card
Register as Associate
