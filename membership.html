Eligibility Criteria

Please review the mandatory statutory qualifications for enrolling in the Forum.

🎂

1. Age Requirement

Any Nigerian not less than eighteen (18) years and not more than thirty-five (35) years of age as at the date of the registration.

🏛️

2. Serving Officer

Is a serving officer in any Ministry, Department or Agency (MDA) of the Federal Government, a State Government or a Local Government Council in Nigeria.

📄

3. Official Verification

Holds a valid letter of employment, appointment or an official Staff identification document from their MDA.

Categories

Classes of Membership

Structured recognition for serving public officers, alumni, and distinguished supporters.

Core Tier

Full Member

A serving civil servant aged 18–35 who has met all eligibility requirements.

Privileges:
  • ✓ Full voting rights in NEC & State elections
  • ✓ Eligibility to hold national & state offices
  • ✓ Priority access to certified capacity building
  • ✓ Direct deployment on Track A national sector teams
Apply as Full Member
Alumni & Advisory

Associate/ Alumni Member

A former full member who has exited on account of age or has left the Civil Service in good standing.

Privileges:
  • ✓ Advisory role on State Working Committees
  • ✓ Faculty access for mentoring young civil servants
  • ✓ Permanent alumni network access
  • ✓ National summit observer credentials
Register as Associate/Alumni
Conferred by NEC

Honorary Member

A distinguished individual conferred with membership by the National Executive Council in recognition of support to the Forum’s objectives.

Privileges:
  • ✓ Strategic council and institutional patronage
  • ✓ Permanent honorific recognition
  • ✓ Keynote addresses at national conventions
